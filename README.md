> This portfolio presents the development of FundiShop, a service marketplace designed to connect homeowners with trusted local service providers known as >Fundis. The five sections work together to explain how our team planned, organised and carried out the project using the Agile methodology. The **Agile Team >Charter** introduces the project, defines the team's roles and establishes how we work together. The **User Stories** describe the needs and expectations of >the users and the features the system should provide. These stories are then organised and prioritised in the **Product Backlog**, while the **Sprint >Backlog** identifies the tasks selected for development during each sprint. Finally, the **Agile Logbook** records our progress, meetings, challenges and >lessons learned throughout the project. Together, these sections demonstrate our project's journey from planning to implementation.
